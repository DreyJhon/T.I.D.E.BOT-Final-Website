CHAPTER I · The Problem and Its Background

This section presents the background, rationale, research locale, conceptual framework, theoretical framework, research questions/objectives, significance, and scope of the T.I.D.E.BOT study. The research focuses on floating solid waste in the coastal waters of Pachoca, Calapan City, Oriental Mindoro.

Research Problem & Rationale

The study focuses on floating solid waste in the shoreline waters of Barangay Pachoca, Calapan City, Oriental Mindoro. T.I.D.E. BOT explores whether an affordable, student-developed robotic platform can support shoreline waste-collection efforts.

01

Local relevance

Pachoca was selected because of recurring floating plastic waste and the community relevance of a waste-collection prototype.

02

Engineering response

The prototype combines buoyancy, propulsion, ultrasonic sensing, microcontroller programming, and an integrated collection net.

Research Framework

The paper uses an Input–Process–Output framework.

INPUT

Biology and Physics concepts, materials and tools, and baseline observations.

PROCESS

Design → Fabrication → Programming → Testing → Analysis.

OUTPUT

Functional and evaluated semi-autonomous surface vehicle.

Performance indicators

Waste collection efficiency, obstacle avoidance accuracy, navigation performance, battery operating time, load-bearing capacity, waterproofing, propulsion, electronics, programming, and frame assembly.

Scientific Principles

Archimedes’ Principle

Supports the explanation of flotation, stability, and load capacity. The paper gives FB = ρVg.

Newton’s Third Law of Motion

The propellers push water backward, and the resulting equal-and-opposite reaction produces forward thrust. This is the appropriate principle for explaining propulsion; Bernoulli’s theorem should not be used as the name for this reaction.

Hydrodynamics

Supports understanding of fluid movement, drag, propulsion, maneuverability, and stability.

Environmental Fluid Mechanics

Provides a framework for how natural water flow can affect the movement and distribution of floating waste.

Scope, Delimitations & Safety

Included

Ocean/shoreline waters of Barangay Pachoca, floating solid waste such as bottles, sachets, food packaging, and wrappers, and the intended collection capacity of the prototype.

Outside the scope

Microplastics, non-solid waste, water-pollution levels, and testing in rivers or lakes.

Environmental care

Minimize disturbance to aquatic life, release accidentally collected organisms, and properly dispose of collected waste.

Field limitations

Weather, wave amplitude, wind, limited time, and budget may affect testing conditions and repeatability.

Research Design, Sampling, Instruments, and Data Analysis
Development Process
01

Needs assessment & concept

Observe shoreline waters in Pachoca and assess the site's solid-waste pollution and testing conditions.

02

Prototype construction

Assemble electronics inside a waterproof enclosure, integrate buoyant materials, and connect the propulsion system.

03

Pre-testing & optimization

Calibrate and check the prototype before formal collection trials.

04

Expert evaluation

Three subject-matter experts evaluate structural stability, mechanical safety, material viability, and operational safety.

05

Field testing

Use standardized target sampling of selected floating solid waste such as bottles, sachets, food packaging, and wrappers.

06

Data analysis

Organize performance observations and summarize experimental data using mean and standard deviation.

Testing Conditions & Controls

The paper describes a consistent test area, starting conditions, procedure, and observation of weather, wind, and waves.

Research integrity: Experimental values remain pending until the physical prototype is tested.
Field Testing and Research Integrity

Standardized target sampling

Use similar types and quantities of floating waste, the same test area and starting point, and a consistent procedure across trials. Record weather, wind, and wave conditions because they can affect performance.

Data gathering sequence

Secure school and local permissions; inspect the prototype; conduct trials with the observation checklist; record collection efficiency, obstacle response, navigation, and operating time; organize the data; and analyze the recorded results.

Statistical treatment

The manuscript proposes the mean and standard deviation across three trials, along with absolute error, percentage error, percentage accuracy, percentage deviation, and percentage precision. Reference standards and final calculations must be completed before results are reported.

Ethics and safety

Check batteries, electronics, propulsion components, and the enclosure before testing. Stop if conditions become unsafe, minimize disturbance to aquatic life, release accidentally collected organisms carefully, dispose of collected waste properly, obtain permissions, and report data honestly.

System Architecture

1 · Sense

Two HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors provide distance information for navigation.

2 · Process

The Arduino Uno receives sensor information and applies the programmed navigation logic.

3 · Actuate

The two ESCs regulate the brushless thrusters in response to control signals from the propulsion-control system.

4 · Collect

The integrated nylon mesh collection net retains selected floating solid waste.
